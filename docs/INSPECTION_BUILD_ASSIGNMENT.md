## Product standard

Open Take Studio is an expressive consumer creation environment. Cinematic media, confident typography, rich depth, purposeful motion and tactile interaction govern this product. The consulting aesthetic of the owner's other brands does not govern this studio. Every active capability needs an understandable entry, correct media presentation, reversible iteration and a useful next action. Infrastructure detail belongs beside provenance and execution, rather than dominating arrival.

The creative stage, role-based references, selected takes, synchronized comparison, frame branches, shot arrangement and export should feel connected. Roles are explicit creative intent: they do not imply unsupported model conditioning or guaranteed identity. Keep motion responsive, suspend hidden media and preserve accessible keyboard/reduced-motion paths.
