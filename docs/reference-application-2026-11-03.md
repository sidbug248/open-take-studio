## Connected product behavior

- `stageTransition` changes actual selected media or instrument state; it interrupts a prior transition and skips travel when reduced motion is requested. There is no animation-based job progress.
- The workspace collapses navigation to one global bar and one project/instrument bar. Image/video/3D actions stay next to the stable preview. One modality selector and collapsed supported settings replace the duplicated controls.
- Recipe cards show an actual recipe result, a related saved example, or a source ingredient with the appropriate label. Named text ingredients and media slots are sent through preflight; the resolved prompt remains inspectable. Local field editing never implies a new generated preview.
- Scene location, time, event, intent, wardrobe, camera and sound are saved story facts. Explicit per-take fact dependencies determine affected outputs. Impact cards show current saved output thumbnails, friendly changed facts and kept source assets; restoring a prior revision keeps all outputs.
- The catalogue has task tools, real saved examples/recipes, and documented model discovery. All 12 families are accessible. Search and readiness/media filters operate on the fetched inventory. A discovered model without a verified adapter has an inspector, not a generation action.
- Original generated/imported references, archive history, existing local tools, shots, comic-page composition, film export, recovered jobs, and project packages remain reachable in the instrument launcher.
