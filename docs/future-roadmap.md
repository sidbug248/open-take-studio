These items remain available for a later development pass. The 0.2.0 closeout focuses on the supported workflows, accurate verification, recovery and packaging. It does not permanently defer or reject further product development, and no date or new spending is committed here.

New work should start from the current application release and preserve the original competition tag. Pick the next opportunity based on creative value, maintenance effort and current provider cost; previous successful checks do not need to be repeated without a concrete reason.
